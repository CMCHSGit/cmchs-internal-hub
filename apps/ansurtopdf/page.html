How it works
  • Each PDF has the same name as its record, ending in .pdf (for example HJ-67031856 - TM80 AS-NZS 3551 PVT 01.10.2026.pdf).
  • Reports show the overall result, the device under test, and every checklist item and measurement with its limits and status. Tests with no recorded results are left out unless you tick “Include tests with no results”.
  • “Open a folder…” and “Save PDFs to folder” work in Edge and Chrome. In other browsers, use “Download all PDFs”, which gives you one PDF, or a .zip when there’s more than one.
  • The .mtr files are only read, never changed.
